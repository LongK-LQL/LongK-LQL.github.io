Research

EP-Flow: Disordered Crystal Structure Prediction without Site-Level Annotations

Qiuliang Liu, Liming Wu, Qi Li, Zhonglong Peng, Chang Chen, Xiaolong Chen, Wenbing Huang, Shifeng Jin

NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted

Formula-conditioned generation of disordered crystal structures. EP-Flow jointly generates occupancies, atomic coordinates, and lattice parameters through flow matching with composition and site-occupancy constraints.

PhaseMatcher: Autoregressive Phase-Set Identification with Spectral Decomposition

Zhonglong Peng, Qiuliang Liu, Chang Chen, Geng Zhong, Qi Li, Lihong Wang, Lan Jiang, Shifeng Jin

arXiv:2610.05844, 2026.10

PhaseMatcher combines phase prediction, spectral decomposition, and stopping decisions in an autoregressive framework for multiphase PXRD analysis. Jointly updated phase contributions and residuals guide sequential phase identification.

AnchorPose for Geometry-Aware MOF Assembly through Meso-Grained Pose Generation

Zhonglong Peng, Rui Jiao, Chang Chen, Geng Zhong, Qiuliang Liu, Shifeng Jin

arXiv:2610.05843, 2026.10

AnchorPose generates periodic MOF assemblies using geometry-aware atomic anchors to represent building-block poses. Alternating anchor generation and rigid reconstruction preserves internal block structures while learning their spatial arrangement.

Generative crystallographic phasing through invariant relationships

Qi Li, Rui Jiao, Liming Wu, Chang Chen, Tiannian Zhu, Bintang Wang, Qiuliang Liu, Zhonglong Peng, Munan Hao, YingPeng Yu, Lin Yao, Wei Ding, Mao Su, Lei Bai, Yang Liu, Hongming Weng, Wenbing Huang, Shifeng Jin, Xiaolong Chen

arXiv:2609.28987, 2026.09

PhiGen learns origin-independent phase relationships to recover diffraction phases. It combines symmetry constraints with generative models for binary phases in centrosymmetric crystals and continuous phases in non-centrosymmetric crystals.

Agentic Fusion of Large Atomic and Language Models to Accelerate Superconductor Discovery

Mingze Li, Yu Rong, Songyou Li, Lihong Wang, Jiacheng Cen, Liming Wu, Anyi Li, Zongzhao Li, Qiuliang Liu, Rui Jiao, Tian Bian, Pengju Wang, Hao Sun, Jianfeng Zhang, Ji-Rong Wen, Deli Zhao, Shifeng Jin, Tingyang Xu, Wenbing Huang

arXiv:2604.23758, 2026.04

ElementsClaw combines large atomic models with language-model reasoning for superconductor discovery. It screens 2.4 million crystals to identify 68,000 high-confidence candidates and guides experimental verification of four new superconductors.
